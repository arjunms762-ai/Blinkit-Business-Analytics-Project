# Blinkit-Business-Analytics-Project
A Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance, outlet trends, product categories, customer ratings, and key business KPIs.

## Objective
The Blinkit Business Analytics Project aimed to analyze grocery sales data to identify key business trends, customer purchasing patterns, and outlet performance. The project involved using Python, SQL, Advanced Excel, and Power BI for data cleaning, analysis, KPI evaluation, and interactive dashboard development. The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions and improve understanding of sales and outlet performance.

### Skills Learned

- Developed the ability to convert raw business data into meaningful insights.
- Improved understanding of sales trends, outlet performance, and product-level analysis
- Gained experience in identifying and interpreting important business KPIs.
- Strengthened data visualization and dashboard storytelling skills.
- Improved ability to communicate analytical findings through clear reports and interactive dashboards
- Developed practical experience in applying multiple analytical techniques to a real-world business dataset.

### Tools Used

- **Python** Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis.
- **SQL** Data extraction, filtering, aggregation, and business analysis.
- **Excel** Data manipulation, calculations, and data validation
- **Power BI** Interactive dashboards, visualizations, and KPI analysis.
- **DAX** Calculated measures and business-specific metrics.
- **Power Query** Data transformation, cleaning, and preparation before analysis.

## Steps


### 1. Data Collection and Understanding
The Blinkit grocery sales dataset was collected and examined to understand the data structure, business fields, and analytical requirements.


### 2. Data Cleaning and Transformation
Power Query was used to import, clean, transform, and prepare the dataset for analysis. Data types and data quality were checked, and the requiredtransformations were performed.


### 3.  Data Analysis Using Python
Python was used for exploratory data analysis to understand sales patterns, product performance, outlet characteristics, and important trends within the dataset.


### 4. SQL Data Analysis
SQL was used to query, filter, aggregate, and analyze the Blinkit data to extract relevant business information and calculate analytical metric.


### 5. DAX and KPI Development
DAX was used in Power Bl to create calculated measures and business KPIs, including total sales, average sales, item count, and average rating.


### 6. Power BI Dashboard Development
Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard containing KPI cards, charts, filters, and visualizations for analyzing sales and outlet performance.


### 7.  Insights and Reporting
The final results from Python, SQL, Power Query, DAX, and Power Bl were combined to identify meaningful business insights and present them through an interactive analytical dashboard.
